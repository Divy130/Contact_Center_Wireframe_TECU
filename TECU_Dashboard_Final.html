📖 Dashboard Glossary & Quick Guide

New to this dashboard? This guide explains the common terms and what they mean in plain English.

📞
Volume (Interactions)

Simple Definition: The number of customer calls, chats, or emails we received in a time period.

Example: "Volume is 7,842" means we handled 7,842 customer contacts in one day/week/month.

Why We Track It: High volume means busy times when we need more staff. Low volume means we're overstaffed.

⏰
SLA (Service Level Agreement)

Simple Definition: A promise we make to customers: "We will answer your call within 2 minutes."

SLA % Meaning: "91% SLA" = 91 out of 100 customers got their call answered on time. 9 had to wait longer than promised.

Target: We aim for 90% or higher. That means at least 9 out of 10 customers get helped on time.

Different SLAs by Channel: Voice (2 min), Chat (1 min), Email (4 hrs)

✅
FCR - First Contact Resolution

Simple Definition: When an agent solves a customer's problem on the FIRST call (no callback needed).

Example: Customer calls → Agent helps → Problem fixed → No follow-up call needed = FCR!

FCR % Meaning: "87% FCR" = 87 out of 100 customers didn't need to call back.

Why It Matters: Customers are happy. We save money (no second call). Target: ≥90%

⏱️
AHT - Average Handling Time

Simple Definition: How long (in minutes) does it take an agent to help one customer?

Example: "AHT is 8 minutes 42 seconds" = On average, each call takes 8.7 minutes to complete.

Why It Matters: Faster isn't always better (might mean poor quality). Slower isn't always bad. Target: ≤7.5 mins per call

Formula: Total time spent on calls ÷ Number of calls = AHT

😊
CSAT - Customer Satisfaction

Simple Definition: How happy customers are after talking to us. Measured on a scale of 1-10.

Example: "CSAT is 8.1 out of 10" = Customers rate their experience 8.1 out of 10. Pretty good!

Target: ≥8.5 out of 10

How We Get It: We send a quick text or email survey after each call asking "How satisfied are you?"

👥
Utilization Rate

Simple Definition: What % of their shift does an agent spend helping customers (vs. idle time)?

Example: "87% utilization" = Agent spends 87% of shift on calls/chats, 13% on breaks/admin.

Healthy Range: 80-90% is optimal. Too low = overstaffed. Too high = agents burning out.

Formula: (Talk time ÷ Available time) × 100 = Utilization %

⚖️
QA Score - Quality Assurance

Simple Definition: How well agents follow company rules and best practices.

What We Check: Greeting ("Hi, thanks for calling!"), Verification (asking for account #), Tone (friendly?), Resolution (did they fix the problem?), Documentation (did they take notes?)

Example: "92% QA Score" = 92 out of 100 calls meet all our standards. Target: ≥90%

How It Works: QA team randomly listens to ~45% of calls and scores them.

📊
Data Scope & Time Frames

What "Data Scope" Means: Which calls are included in this number?

Example Scopes:
• "All Channels" = Voice + Chat + Email calls combined
• "Last 30 Days" = June 1 - July 1, 2024
• "Current Shift Only" = Agents working RIGHT NOW

Why It Matters: You can't compare "all channels" with "voice only" - different numbers!

Always Check the Filter: Click "Channel" or "Date Range" to see exactly what data you're looking at.

📈
Reading Percentages Correctly

The Confusing Part: "91% SLA improved 3%" - does that mean 94.73%? NO!

What It Actually Means: Last period was 88%, this period is 91%. That's a 3 percentage point increase.

Why? Percentages are already max'd at 100%. You can't increase 91% by 5% to get 95.55% - doesn't make sense operationally.

Always Read It Like This: "↑ 3% vs target" means "3 points higher than target" not "multiply by 1.03"

🔮
How We Predict Future Volume

Simple Explanation: We look at the past 30 days of volume patterns and predict tomorrow's traffic.

Example: "Tuesdays between 10-11am always get 150+ calls" → We predict next Tuesday same thing

What Affects Predictions: Day of week, time of day, holidays, weather, recent trends

How Accurate? Usually 80-90% accurate. Used to schedule staff.

Where You'll See It: "24-Hour Volume Forecast" chart in Real-time tab

📚 Key Metrics Definitions

⚠️ Important Notes on KPI Comparisons & Reading the Data:

  • Volume & SLA Comparison: We compare Volume (number of customer interactions) with SLA (% of interactions answered on time). Why? High volume doesn't automatically mean poor SLA - we monitor both to understand if we're handling more calls while maintaining quality.
  • Percentages vs. Percentages: When we say "SLA improved 3%", this means the metric moved from 88% to 91% (3 percentage points higher). It does NOT mean "91% × 1.03 = 93.73%".
  • Last Period Definition: "Last period" means the previous 60 days before the current reporting period. Current period: June 1 - July 31, 2024. Previous period: April 2 - June 1, 2024.
  • Data Scope: All KPIs include all channels (Voice, Chat, Email) unless otherwise noted. Use filters above to drill down by specific channel or queue.
📞
SLA Compliance

SLA: Service Level Agreement - A commitment to answer customer interactions within a specified timeframe.

Definition: Percentage of interactions answered or responded to within the defined SLA timeframe set by the organization.

Formula: (Interactions answered within SLA time / Total interactions) × 100

Data Scope: All channels | Target: ≥90% | Range: Voice: 2min, Chat: 1min, Email: 4hrs

⏱️
Average Handling Time (AHT)

Definition: Average time agents spend on handling a single customer interaction (call, chat, or email).

Formula: Total handling time / Number of interactions

Data Scope: All channels combined | Target: ≤7.5 mins | Unit: Minutes:Seconds | Excludes: After-call work time

✅
First Contact Resolution (FCR)

Definition: Percentage of customer issues resolved on the first interaction without requiring follow-up or callback.

Formula: (Issues resolved on first contact / Total interactions) × 100 = FCR %

Example: If 870 out of 1,000 interactions are resolved on first contact: (870 ÷ 1,000) × 100 = 87% FCR

Why It Matters: Higher FCR means customers get answers faster and don't have to call back. Industry standard is 75-85%, our target is ≥90%.

Data Scope: All channels | Target: ≥90% | Measured by: Agent notes + follow-up ticket tracking

😊
CSAT Score

Definition: Customer Satisfaction Score - measures member satisfaction on a 10-point scale.

Calculation: Post-call survey rating average

Data Scope: All channels | Scale: 1-10 (10=Excellent) | Target: ≥8.5 | Response Rate: ~35% of interactions

👥
Agent Utilization

Definition: Percentage of time agents spend handling interactions vs. total available time.

Formula: (Talk time / Available time) × 100

Data Scope: Current shift only (real-time) | Target: 80-90% (optimal range) | Includes: Talk + Hold time | Excludes: Breaks & training

⚖️
Quality Assurance (QA) Score

Definition: Overall compliance score based on call quality audits across multiple criteria.

Criteria: Greeting, verification, tone, resolution, documentation

Data Scope: Random sample of calls (45% coverage last 30 days) | Target: ≥90% | Audited by: QA team, Compliance & Supervisors | Review Time: 24-48hrs post-call
